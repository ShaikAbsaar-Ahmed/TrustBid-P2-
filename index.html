Pipeline

From Upload to Verdict in Four Steps

A hybrid pipeline: fast digital text extraction first, OCR only when a page is truly a scan — then deterministic, explainable rules make the call.

01
📤

Smart Document Upload

Bidder certificates are uploaded per document type and stored in an encrypted, audit-locked repository.

02
🔍

AI Extraction & OCR

PyMuPDF reads digital PDFs instantly; Tesseract OCR handles scanned pages. AI extracts registration numbers, dates, PAN & GSTIN — with the source sentence kept as evidence.

03
⚖️

Rule Matching & Risk Score

Every field is checked against the tender's rule set: expiry, cross-document consistency, verified-registry match and fraud red flags. Each check adds risk points.

04
✅

Verdict + Report

Pass, Fail or Needs Review — with a per-bidder risk score, highlighted evidence, a downloadable compliance report and a full audit trail.
